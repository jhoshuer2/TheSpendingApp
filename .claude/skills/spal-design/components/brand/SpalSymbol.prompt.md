Hairline brand mark — use in place of illustrations for empty states, loading, onboarding and section breaks.

```jsx
<SpalSymbol symbol="profit" size={72} />
<SpalSymbol symbol="flow" spin />   {/* loading */}
```

- Symbols: profit · flow · growth · focus · consistency · momentum · insight · cashflow.
- 1.25 stroke in ink (`color`) with exactly one accent point (`accent`, default green). Never filled, never multicolor.
- Pair with the horizon atmosphere (`--atmos-dawn` / `--atmos-night`) on onboarding and empty states.
