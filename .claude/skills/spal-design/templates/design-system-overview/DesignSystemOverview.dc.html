The canvas
Not white. Not gray.
Every screen sits on a soft sage-green tint. It is the cheapest possible signature and the most effective one — the tint alone reads as SPAL, and it makes white cards feel like objects rather than background.

Spacing & radius
Tight inside, generous between.
4–12px gaps within a component, 24–32px between sections, a constant 20px page margin. The contrast is what reads as considered rather than padded.
Seven radius steps — each a distinct size class, never one blanket rounding

Depth
Layered, not heavy.
Every shadow is two co-authored layers: a tight near-black contact shadow and a soft, larger, low-opacity ambient falloff. Never a single heavy drop shadow.

Motion
Flow, never bounce.
Entrances fade and rise 8–12px, staggered 40ms apart. Real spring physics are banned. Press states are tactile, not chromatic — nothing darkens on tap.
Default easing
cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1) · 220ms
Entrances

Delight — onboarding only

Press scale

Voice
A coach, not a spreadsheet.
Plain WhatsApp-level language, held to the three-second rule. Accounting jargon is banned outright: expenditure, revenue, liabilities, assets, ledger, reconcile.
Write this
“You spent more money than you made today.”
“Your Sales Today” · “Mark paid” · “No activity yet today” · “Head Back Home”
Not this
“Your expenditure exceeded your revenue.”
“Reconcile ledger” · “Liabilities summary” · “No records found” · “Return to dashboard”
